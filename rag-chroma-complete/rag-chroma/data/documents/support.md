# Atlas Kitchen — Support and access policy
This is fictional documentation for the RAG demonstration.

Standard support is available Monday through Friday from 09:00 to 17:00 Europe/Rome time. Critical incidents should include the gateway identifier, UTC incident timestamp, and the most recent application logs with secrets removed.

Readers may view telemetry dashboards. Operators may acknowledge alerts. Administrators may manage device registrations. Service account credentials must not be stored in document repositories or application logs.

Telemetry is retained for 90 days in the operational database. Backup retention is 30 days. The documentation does not specify product pricing, employee salaries, or customer discounts.
